# B/X D&D Quick Reference for DM

This document contains the essential rules for running B/X D&D (Basic/Expert). Reference this for all mechanical rulings.

---

## COMBAT SEQUENCE

Combat follows this STRICT order every round:

### 1. INITIATIVE (Roll Every Round)
- Each side rolls 1d6
- Higher roll acts first
- Ties: actions are simultaneous
- **ALWAYS roll initiative before any combat actions**

### 2. COMBAT ROUND ORDER
The side that wins initiative completes ALL of these steps first:

1. **Morale Check** (if applicable - first blood, half casualties)
2. **Movement** (up to encounter speed)
3. **Missile Attacks** (bows, crossbows, thrown weapons)
4. **Magic** (spell casting)
5. **Melee Attacks** (swords, axes, etc.)

Then the losing side takes their turn in the same order.

### 3. ATTACK ROLLS
- Roll 1d20 + modifiers
- Compare to target's AC using THAC0
- **To Hit = THAC0 - Target AC** (roll this or higher)
- Natural 20 always hits
- Natural 1 always misses

### THAC0 BY CLASS AND LEVEL
| Level | Fighter | Cleric/Thief | Magic-User |
|-------|---------|--------------|------------|
| 1-3   | 19      | 19           | 19         |
| 4-6   | 17      | 19           | 19         |
| 7-9   | 15      | 17           | 19         |
| 10-12 | 13      | 15           | 17         |
| 13-15 | 11      | 13           | 15         |

### ARMOR CLASS
| Armor | AC |
|-------|-----|
| None | 9 |
| Leather | 7 |
| Chain Mail | 5 |
| Plate Mail | 3 |
| Shield | -1 (bonus) |

## SAVING THROWS

When a character faces magical or dangerous effects, they roll 1d20 vs their saving throw.

### SAVING THROW CATEGORIES
1. **Death/Poison** - Instant death effects, poison
2. **Wands** - Wand effects, device attacks
3. **Paralysis/Petrify** - Paralysis, petrification, hold
4. **Breath Weapon** - Dragon breath, area effects
5. **Spells/Rods/Staves** - Direct spell effects

### SAVING THROWS BY CLASS (Level 1-3)

|-------|-------|-------|-----------|--------|--------|

## MORALE

Monsters and NPCs check morale at these times:
1. **First death** on their side
2. **Half the group** is killed or incapacitated

### MORALE CHECK
- Roll 2d6
- If roll > Morale score, they flee/surrender
- Morale 12 = never flees (fanatic)
- Morale 2 = flees immediately

### COMMON MORALE SCORES
| Type | Morale |
|------|--------|
| Untrained/Peasant | 6 |
| Regular Soldier | 8 |
| Elite/Veteran | 9 |
| Fanatic/Undead | 12 |
| Animals (wild) | 7 |
| Animals (trained) | 9 |

## MOVEMENT

### EXPLORATION (Dungeon)
- **Exploration Speed**: 120' per turn (10 minutes)
- **Encounter Speed**: 40' per round (for 120' base)
- **Running Speed**: 120' per round (3x encounter speed)

### MOVEMENT RATES BY ARMOR
| Armor | Base | Exploration | Encounter |
|-------|------|-------------|-----------|
| None/Light | 120' | 120'/turn | 40'/round |
| Chain | 90' | 90'/turn | 30'/round |
| Plate | 60' | 60'/turn | 20'/round |

### ENCUMBRANCE
Carrying too much reduces movement:
- Light load: Normal movement
- Medium load: -30' base
- Heavy load: -60' base

## EXPLORATION PROCEDURES

### DUNGEON TURNS (10 minutes each)
Each turn, characters can:
- Move up to exploration speed
- Search a 10'x10' area
- Listen at a door
- Pick a lock
- Other careful activities

### WANDERING MONSTERS
- Check every 2 turns (roll 1d6)
- Encounter on 1
- Also check when party makes loud noise

### DOORS
- Most dungeon doors are stuck
- Open: Roll 1d6, success on 1-2 (or with STR bonus)
- Doors close automatically unless spiked
- Monsters can open doors freely

### SECRET DOORS
- Found on 1 in 6 (1-2 for Elves)
- Requires actively searching
- Each 10' section takes 1 turn to search

### LISTEN AT DOOR
- Success on 1 in 6 (1-2 for Elves, Halflings)

## LIGHT AND VISION

### LIGHT SOURCES
| Source | Radius | Duration |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Torch | 30' | 1 hour (6 turns) |
| Lantern | 30' | 4 hours (24 turns) |
| Candle | 5' | 1 hour |

### INFRAVISION
- Dwarves, Elves, Halflings: 60'
- Sees heat signatures, not colors
- Blocked by light sources

## REACTION ROLLS

When encountering intelligent creatures with unclear intentions:

### REACTION TABLE (2d6)
| Roll | Reaction |
|------|----------|
| 2 | Immediate attack |
| 3-5 | Hostile, may attack |
| 6-8 | Uncertain, wary |
| 9-11 | No attack, may negotiate |
| 12 | Enthusiastic, helpful |

### MODIFIERS
- +1 per point of CHA bonus
- Situational modifiers as appropriate

---

## HEALING AND REST

### NATURAL HEALING
- 1d3 HP per full day of rest
- No rest = no healing

### MAGICAL HEALING
- Cure Light Wounds: 1d6+1 HP
- Potions, scrolls as specified

### DEATH AND DYING
- 0 HP = dead (no negative HP in B/X)
- Some DMs allow -10 for dramatic purposes

---

## SURPRISE

### CHECKING FOR SURPRISE
- Each side rolls 1d6
- Surprised on 1-2
- Surprised creatures cannot act for 1 round
- If one side surprised, other gets free round

### MODIFIERS
- Halflings: Surprise on 1 only (harder to surprise them)
- Certain monsters have special surprise chances

---

## COMMON SITUATIONS

### FALLING
- 1d6 damage per 10' fallen

### DROWNING
- Can hold breath for CON rounds
- Then save vs Death each round or die

### FIRE
- Torch: 1d4 damage
- Oil (burning): 1d8 damage for 2 rounds
- Larger fires: 1d6 per round exposed

### POISON
- Usually save vs Poison or die
- Some poisons deal damage instead

## EXPERIENCE POINTS

### XP SOURCES
1. **Monsters defeated**: XP value in stat block
2. **Treasure recovered**: 1 XP per 1 GP value
   - Must be brought back to safety
   - Treasure is primary XP source (75%+)

### LEVEL ADVANCEMENT
Characters level up when XP total reaches threshold for their class.

---

## HIRELINGS AND RETAINERS

### RETAINERS
- Loyal followers who adventure with party
- Max number = CHA modifier + 4
- Get half share of XP and treasure
- Check morale in dangerous situations

### HIRELINGS
- Hired for specific services (guards, porters)
- Don't get XP
- Won't enter dungeons (usually)

---

## DM REMINDERS

### ALWAYS DO THESE
1. **Roll initiative every round** - don't skip this
2. **Check morale** when enemies take first casualty and at 50% losses
3. **Track light sources** - torches last 1 hour
4. **Check for wandering monsters** every 2 turns
5. **Apply encumbrance** if carrying heavy loads

### COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
- Letting combat start without initiative
- Forgetting morale checks (enemies should flee sometimes)
- Ignoring light/darkness in dungeons
- Not tracking time and resources
- Making treasure too scarce (it's the main XP source)

### RULINGS OVER RULES
When in doubt:
- Roll d6: 1-2 = unfavorable, 3-4 = mixed, 5-6 = favorable
- Or roll d20 under relevant ability score
- Keep the game moving; note ruling for consistency
